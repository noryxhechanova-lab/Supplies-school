About us

Who we are and why we sell school supplies.

We are a group of students who created Brightside School Supplies for our class project. We chose school supplies because every student uses them every day. Our goal is to offer useful, good-looking items at fair prices, so everyone can start the school year ready.

  • Items students really use
  • Colors and designs you will enjoy
  • Low prices students can afford
  • Safe, non-toxic art supplies
